summary: From Tehran Sonnati Radif to Nawahi regional traditions across Iran.

---

Beyond classical *Mūsīqī-e Sonnati*, regional traditions (*Mūsīqī-e Nawāḥī*) preserve distinct modal systems, tunings, and bardic repertoires across Khorāsān, Kurdistān, Āzarbāijān, Lorestān, Balūchistān, and the Persian Gulf.
