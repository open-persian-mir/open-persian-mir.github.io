Persian musical instruments span chordophones (*Tār, Setār, Santūr, Kamāncheh, Barbat, Qānūn, Ṭanbūr, Dūtār*), aerophones (*Ney, Sornā, Balabān*), membranophones (*Tombak, Daf, Dāyereh*), and the classical voice (*Āvāz*).
