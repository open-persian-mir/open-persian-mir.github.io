Unlike 12-tone equal temperament (12-TET), Persian classical music uses flexible neutral intervals denoted via **Koron** (half-flat) and **Sori** (half-sharp).

## Systematist Whole Tone Formula

$$
\text{Whole Tone } (T) = 2L + C = \frac{9}{8} \approx 203.91\text{ cents}
$$

| Interval | Ratio | Approx. Cents | Role |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Limma | $256:243$ | $90.22\text{¢}$ | Minor second |
| Neutral Second | $12:11$ | $150.64\text{¢}$ | Shur / Segah microtone |
| Whole Tone | $9:8$ | $203.91\text{¢}$ | Major second |
