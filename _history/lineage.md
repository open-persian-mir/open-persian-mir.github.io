---
title: Historical & Theoretical Lineage
summary: From Islamic Golden Age theorists to Qajar Radif codification and modern computational analysis.
---

Persian music theory spans early mathematical treatises (such as Al-Farabi, Ibn Sina, and Safi al-Din al-Urmawi), the codification of the *Radif* in the Qajar era, 20th-century notation reforms, and modern computational MIR research.
